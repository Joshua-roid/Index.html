The History

Fried chicken has ancient roots, with early versions of fried meats appearing in the Middle Ages, but the dish we recognize today developed in the American South. Scots were the first recorded to deep fry chicken in fat in the 1700s, using simple seasoning, while West African culinary traditions contributed bold spices and frying techniques that transformed the dish into the flavorful Southern fried chicken known today. Over time, this fusion of Scottish frying and African seasoning became a staple of Southern cuisine and eventually spread across the United States and the world, becoming one of the most iconic comfort foods in American culture.

Ingredients
  • 2 lbs chicken pieces
  • 2 cups buttermilk
  • 1 tbsp hot sauce
  • 2 cups flour
  • Spices: salt, pepper, paprika, garlic powder
  • Oil for frying
Recipe
  • Step 1: Marinate chicken in buttermilk and spices (4+ hours)
  • Step 2:Mix flour, cornstarch, and spices for dredge
  • Step 3: Coat chicken in flour mixture
  • Step 4 Heat oil to 350°F and fry in batches
  • Step 5 Drain on rack and rest before serving.
